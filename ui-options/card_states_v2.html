Keep plain text. There are only ever two lines, and each already opens with a bold lead, so the bold does the scanning a bullet would. Bullets add indent and visual weight, and they break down in the Activating state where there is one sentence, not a list. They only earn their place at three or more parallel items.

Rule to keep it consistent: line 1 is what the method is, line 2 is why it pays off. Active and Available show both lines. Activating swaps them for what is happening and what happens next.
