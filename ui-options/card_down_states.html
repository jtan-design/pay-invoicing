Card payments down: what the payee and the payer see

Two different errors, two different colours. Action needed (red) means the payee must do something. Unavailable (amber) means the problem is on our side or our partner's, and the payee can only wait. Same tile skeleton for both. Mock-up only, Figma untouched.
