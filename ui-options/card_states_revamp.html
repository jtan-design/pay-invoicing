Get Paid overview: payment method states

Revamp of the Bank transfer and Card tiles, shaped with the design-taste checklist for app UI (Operate mode: restrained colour, one state vocabulary, one action per tile, no decoration that does not carry state). Mock-up only. Nothing changed in Figma.

1. One status slotSame chip position and four fixed meanings on both tiles: Available, Activating, Active, Action needed.
2. Colour means stateInactive tiles stay neutral. Blue is for the one action and the single "not yet on" nudge. Green only ever means live.
3. No banner inside a cardThe "Give customers more ways to pay" strip nested a card in a card. It becomes the tile's lead line plus a soft ring.
4. Same skeleton, same heightIcon, name, status, one fact, one footer action in a fixed slot. The pair always reads as equals.
5. Copy trimmedOne plain sentence per state. Typo fixed ("More ways to way"). Sentence case, no exclamation marks.
