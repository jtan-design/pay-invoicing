Replacing the blue band on "Accept card payments" and "Accept bank transfer"

Feedback: the blue band reads as a selected state, even in a lighter blue. That is a fair read. Across the product, a blue outline with a tinted fill means selected (your Accepting payment method cards use exactly that). So the band borrows a meaning it should not have. Options below. Mock-up only.

Recommendation
Go with A, keep C in reserve. The heading already says "Give customers more ways to pay", so the band is redundant as well as misleading. Start with the tile exactly like the active ones plus the "Available" pill. If card activation is low in testing, add the quiet line from C rather than bringing back a coloured wrapper. This also applies to the Bank tile when card is the active method, and to E4 in the new turn-off flow.
